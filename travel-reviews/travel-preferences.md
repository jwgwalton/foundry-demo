# Travel Preferences

## Traveller Profile

Prefers trips that are easy to navigate on foot, with good public transport,
independent restaurants, food markets, coffee stops, viewpoints and compact
neighbourhoods. Likes having a loose plan with optional alternatives rather than
a packed schedule.

## Weather Fit

Best travel weather is mild and comfortable for walking. Ideal daytime
temperature is 18-24C. Temperatures above 30C reduce enjoyment unless the trip
has beaches, shaded routes or long indoor breaks. Light rain is acceptable when
there are good museums, restaurants or indoor markets nearby. Heavy rain, high
humidity or strong wind should lower the recommendation.

## Trip Style

Likes shoulder-season travel, local neighbourhoods, independent hotels, scenic
walks, public squares, bookshops, galleries and low-friction evenings. Prefers
four to five night trips where the first day can be relaxed.

## Things To Avoid

Avoid overly crowded tourist cores, long airport transfers, car-dependent areas,
very early starts on consecutive days, expensive resort areas and destinations
where most attractions require timed reservations far in advance.

## Scoring Guidance

Increase the fit score when a destination has mild weather, strong food culture,
walkable districts, reliable transit and good indoor alternatives. Reduce the
score for extreme heat, heavy rain, unclear transport, event-driven crowding or a
trip plan that depends on too many bookings.
