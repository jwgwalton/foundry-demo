# Lisbon, October 2023

## Summary

Four-night solo trip based near Principe Real. The trip worked well because the
city was walkable, evenings were mild, and there were enough cafes, viewpoints
and restaurants to keep the plan flexible.

## What Worked

-   Principe Real and Chiado were good bases for walking without feeling too
    isolated at night.
-   Late afternoons at viewpoints were a highlight.
-   Food markets and small restaurants made rainy spells easy to work around.
-   Public transport was useful, but the best parts of the trip were short walks
    between neighbourhoods.

## What Did Not Work

-   Some steep routes became tiring when trying to fit in too much.
-   The busiest parts of Baixa and the tram routes felt less enjoyable at peak
    times.
-   A day with persistent rain needed a slower indoor plan.

## Weather Notes

Comfortable when daytime temperatures were around 20-23C and evenings were cool
but pleasant. Light rain was acceptable because there were indoor food and
culture options nearby.

## Future Recommendation

Lisbon is a strong fit in October if the forecast is mild and rain risk is not
dominant. Prefer staying central, planning one main neighbourhood per day and
keeping a flexible indoor backup.
