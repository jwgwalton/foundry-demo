# Seville, March 2025

## Summary

Four-night trip built around food, architecture and evening walks. Seville was
excellent in mild weather but would be less appealing in high heat.

## What Worked

-   Santa Cruz and Triana were memorable walking areas.
-   Evenings were the best time for wandering, plazas and tapas.
-   The city rewarded a relaxed pace with long breaks between major sights.
-   Warm but not hot weather made outdoor meals and walks enjoyable.

## What Did Not Work

-   Midday sun was tiring even outside peak summer.
-   Some major sights needed timed planning.
-   The trip would have been much weaker with temperatures above 30C.

## Weather Notes

Ideal daytime range was about 20-26C with cooler evenings. Heat risk should be a
major factor for later spring, summer or early autumn trips.

## Future Recommendation

Seville is a very strong fit in spring or late autumn when temperatures are mild.
Avoid periods with high heat, limited shade or a schedule that requires walking
long distances at midday.
