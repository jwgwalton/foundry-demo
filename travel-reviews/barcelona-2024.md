# Barcelona, May 2024

## Summary

Five-night trip focused on food, architecture and neighbourhood walks. The city
was enjoyable, but crowd levels and timed reservations affected spontaneity.

## What Worked

-   Eixample was a practical base with good transit and easy walking routes.
-   Early evening walks, markets and casual restaurants were the best parts of
    the trip.
-   Mild weather made long walking days comfortable.
-   Having one reserved attraction per day worked better than packing the
    schedule.

## What Did Not Work

-   The busiest attractions required more advance planning than preferred.
-   Crowds around major sights reduced enjoyment.
-   Moving between beach, old town and hill viewpoints in one day made the day
    feel fragmented.

## Weather Notes

Comfortable around 21-25C. Direct sun felt tiring by mid-afternoon, so shaded
routes and breaks were useful.

## Future Recommendation

Barcelona is a good fit in shoulder season if the plan limits major attractions
and leaves evenings open for food, walking and neighbourhood exploration.
